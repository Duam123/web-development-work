Before You Book

A few things people usually ask

Do I need a doctor's referral?+

No referral needed for a general assessment. Post-surgical patients should bring their surgeon's notes if available, so we can coordinate the protocol.

How many sessions will I need?+

It depends on the injury — a sprain might need 4-6 sessions, while post-surgical rehab can run 8-16 weeks. Your first assessment gives a realistic estimate.

Do you offer home visits?+

Yes — for patients who can't travel, we offer full home visit sessions across Lahore. Ask about availability when you call.

What's your cancellation policy?+

Give us at least 4 hours' notice so we can offer the slot to someone else — no charge for cancellations made ahead of time.
