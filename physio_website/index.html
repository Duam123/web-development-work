The Recovery Journey

What actually happens, visit to visit

The same four stages whether you're here for a sprained ankle or a spinal fusion.

01

Assess

Range of motion, strength testing, and a pain map — your baseline gets measured, not guessed.

02

Plan

A therapist builds a week-by-week plan matched to your specific injury and goals, not a generic sheet.

03

Treat

Hands-on therapy, guided exercise, and modalities like ultrasound or dry needling where they help.

04

Progress

Re-measured range of motion and strength at each milestone, so your plan adjusts to real data.
