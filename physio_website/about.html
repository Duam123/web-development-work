Our Story

Started in Lahore, built around measurable recovery.

Restore Motion opened in 2011 with one habit that hasn't changed since: measure range of motion and strength at every visit, so a recovery plan is judged by numbers, not just how someone says they feel.

Measured, not assumed

Range of motion and strength are tracked at every visit — progress is a number, not a feeling.

One therapist, whole plan

You keep the same therapist through your recovery — no re-explaining your case every visit.

Coordinated with your surgeon

Post-surgical plans follow your surgeon's protocol directly — we don't improvise around it.
