Before You Book

A few things people usually ask

01
Do I need a referral to book an appointment?+

No referral needed for a general consultation or AI skin analysis. Some insurance plans may require one for specialist follow-up — ask when you call.

02
Is the AI skin analysis a substitute for seeing a dermatologist?+

No — it's a data point. A board-certified dermatologist always reviews the scan and examines you in person before anything is diagnosed or treated.

03
How often should I get a mole screening?+

Once a year for most people, more often if you have a personal or family history of skin cancer — your dermatologist will recommend a schedule at your first visit.

04
Do you accept insurance?+

Medical dermatology visits are often covered under provincial or private insurance; cosmetic treatments typically aren't. We'll confirm coverage before you're billed.

05
What's your cancellation policy?+

Give us at least 4 hours' notice so we can offer the slot to someone else — no charge for cancellations made ahead of time.
