Our Story

Built by dermatologists tired of the 6-minute appointment.

Claria opened in 2018 with one rule: every patient gets a real assessment before anything gets prescribed or performed. The AI scan didn't replace that rule — it just makes it faster.

9,400+
Skin assessments run since 2018 — every one reviewed by a board-certified dermatologist.

Diagnosis before treatment

The AI scan gives data, not a verdict — a dermatologist always reviews it and examines you in person.

02

Numbers you can track

Every scan is saved, so your next visit compares against your own baseline — not a generic chart.

03

No treatment you don't need
