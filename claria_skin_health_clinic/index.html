How A Visit Works

Screen, diagnose, treat, monitor

The same four steps whether you're here for a mole check or a breakout.

01

Screen

A quick AI-assisted scan and intake, so your dermatologist starts with data instead of a guess.

02

Diagnose

A board-certified dermatologist reviews the scan and examines in person before naming anything.

03

Treat

A plan matched to what was actually found — medical, cosmetic, or both, in the right order.

04

Monitor

Follow-up scans track real change over time, so we know a plan is working instead of assuming.
